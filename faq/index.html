Pool & Spa Electrical

Does a new pool pump need GFCI protection?

Yes — when a pool pump is replaced, code requires upgrading to GFCI protection. We include it when we wire the new pump.

Do you install pool heat pumps?

Yes. We install GulfStream pool heat pumps and diagnose and repair heat pump power and control problems. See also heat pump & AC electrical.

Can you wire my Pentair automation system?

Yes — automation wiring, including Pentair panels, is regular work for us.

Do you work with pool contractors?

Yes. We're the electrical trade partner for local pool contractors on new builds and equipment changes.

EV Chargers

Hardwired charger or a 240V outlet — which is better?

Both work. A hardwired wall charger (like a Tesla wall connector) is tidy; a 240V receptacle such as a NEMA 14-50 lets you use the charger that came with your car. We'll help you choose.

Will my panel handle an EV charger?

We check before installing. If there isn't room, options include a load calculation, a smaller charger setting or a panel upgrade.

Do you install 240V outlets for RVs or workshops?

Yes.

Generator Interlocks & Surge Protection

What is a generator interlock?

A listed kit that mounts on your panel and mechanically prevents the main breaker and generator breaker from being on at the same time, so you can't backfeed the utility lines.

What does whole-home surge protection do?

A whole-home SPD installs at your main panel and helps protect everything downstream — AC equipment, pool automation, appliances and electronics.

Do I still need surge strips?

Point-of-use surge strips are still a good idea for sensitive electronics. They work alongside whole-home protection.
