What we do

We provide maintenance and repair electrical work, common-area and parking lot lighting, exterior and walkway lighting, exit and emergency lighting, pool and amenity electrical, and troubleshooting for managed properties. We also work as the electrical subcontractor for local pool contractors. Property managers and boards get clear communication, written quotes and invoices they can pay online.

  • Condominium associations and HOAs: common-area, walkway, parking and exterior lighting; exit and emergency lighting; amenity and pool electrical
  • Apartments and condo communities: campus lighting, common-area and building electrical, maintenance and repairs
  • Mobile-home communities: mobile-home pedestals
  • Commercial plazas: maintenance, lighting, repairs, tenant-space circuits and troubleshooting
  • Trade partner for pool contractors: pool and spa panels, equipment feeds, heat pumps (GulfStream installs), Pentair automation wiring, bonding and GFCI
  • Panels, subpanels and service equipment repair or replacement
  • AC/heat pump disconnects and soft starts across multiple units
  • Permits pulled and inspections scheduled
  • Written quotes; invoices sent by QuickBooks with an online payment link
