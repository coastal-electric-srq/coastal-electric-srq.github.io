What we do

Before we install anything, we check whether your panel and service have room for the new load. If they don't, we'll walk you through the options, such as a load calculation, a smaller charger setting or a panel upgrade. We make sure the circuit, breaker and wire size match the charger's requirements. We also handle 240V outlets for RVs and workshops.

  • Hardwired Level 2 wall chargers, including Tesla wall connectors
  • 240V receptacles (e.g., NEMA 14-50) for the charger that came with your car
  • Panel and service capacity check before install
  • Options when the panel is tight: load calculation, smaller charger setting or panel upgrade
  • 240V outlets for RV hookups and workshops
  • Whole-home surge protection add-on to protect your investment

How an EV charger install works

  1. Text us 2 photos: your panel and the spot where you want the charger — usually enough for a first estimate.
  2. Capacity check: we confirm your panel and service have room, or explain the options.
  3. Install: circuit, breaker and wire sized to the charger's requirements.
  4. Test: we confirm it charges before we leave.

Common questions

Hardwired charger or a 240V outlet — which is better?

Both work. A hardwired wall charger (like a Tesla wall connector) is tidy; a 240V receptacle such as a NEMA 14-50 lets you use the charger that came with your car. We'll help you choose.

Will my panel handle an EV charger?

We check before installing. If there isn't room, options include a load calculation, a smaller charger setting or a panel upgrade.

Do you install 240V outlets for RVs or workshops?

Yes.
