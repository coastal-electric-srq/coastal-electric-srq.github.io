Services

Specialty

Pool & Spa Electrical

Pool and spa work is the biggest part of what we do. Panels, GFCI and bonding, variable-speed pumps, automation and pool heat pumps — wired to last in Suncoast heat, rain and salt air.

Heat Pump & AC Electrical

AC and heat pump systems run hard in Southwest Florida, and the electrical parts outside take a beating. We replace failing disconnects, repair the circuits and install soft starts.

Panel & Service Upgrades

Older Sarasota-area homes often still have original panels, undersized services or corroded equipment. We size the upgrade properly instead of guessing.

EV Chargers

Ready to charge at home? We install Level 2 (240-volt) EV charging — a hardwired wall charger, including Tesla wall connectors, or a 240V receptacle such as a NEMA 14-50.

Generator Interlocks & Surge Protection

Run selected circuits safely from a portable generator when the power's out — and protect your AC, pool automation and appliances from surges.

HOA, Commercial & Condo

Condominium associations and condo communities, HOAs, apartments, mobile-home communities, commercial plazas and trade partners get a dependable electrician with clear communication, written quotes and easy invoicing.

Lighting

Recessed lighting, LED retrofits, cabinet lighting, ceiling fans, exterior and security lighting, and smart controls.

Troubleshooting & repairs

We fix outlets or circuits that stopped working, tripping breakers, flickering lights, failed GFCIs and burned connections. We trace the problem to its source and explain what we found.
