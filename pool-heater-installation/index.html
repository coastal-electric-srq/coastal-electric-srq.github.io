What the electrical side of a pool heat pump involves

We also track down heat pumps that trip breakers or won't power up, and we work alongside local pool companies. Every pool heat pump install needs the same electrical basics:

  • A dedicated 240-volt circuit from the panel, sized to the unit
  • A maintenance disconnect within sight of the equipment. The electrical code requires it to be at least 5 feet from the water, unless there's a permanent barrier.
  • Wiring methods suited to a wet, corrosive equipment pad
  • A tie-in to the pool's bonding
  • GFCI protection on the circuit
  • The permit and inspection

How we size the breaker and wire

Every pool heat pump's data plate lists its minimum circuit ampacity (MCA) and its maximum overcurrent protection, the largest breaker allowed. We size the wire and breaker from those numbers, the distance to the panel and the installation manual. Amps vary a lot from model to model, so we don't quote one number for every unit. Never upsize a breaker to stop it tripping. A breaker bigger than the data plate allows isn't safe.

Does your panel have room?

Before we add the circuit, we check your panel's free spaces and your home's overall load. If the panel is full, old or undersized, we explain the options, such as a subpanel or a panel or service upgrade.

When a pool heat pump keeps tripping the breaker or won't power up

The electrical causes are often loose or corroded connections at the pad, a failing breaker, a damaged wire, or moisture in the disconnect or whip. Some trips come from the unit itself, like the motor, compressor or control board. We test the power side, diagnose and repair heat pump power and control problems, and tell you what we found. Don't keep resetting a breaker that keeps tripping, and don't swap in a bigger breaker. For AC and HVAC heat pump circuits, see heat pump & AC electrical.

Working with your pool company

We're the electrical trade partner for local pool contractors on new builds and equipment changes. We install all major brands of pool heat pumps, including GulfStream. Permits and inspections

A new pool heat pump circuit is permitted electrical work. Who issues the permit depends on where your home is: the county, or a city with its own building department, and Lakewood Ranch sits in two counties. We handle the permit with the building department that covers your address when the job needs one.

Salt air and the equipment pad

Coastal air is hard on connections at the pool pad. While we're there, we check the existing disconnect and connections for corrosion.

Pool heater installation questions

How many amps does a pool heat pump draw?

It depends on the model. The data plate lists the minimum circuit ampacity (MCA) and the maximum breaker size, and we size the circuit from those numbers. Text us a photo of the data plate.

What size breaker does a pool heat pump need?

The size the data plate and the installation manual call for, and never larger. Amps vary a lot from model to model, so we don't quote one number for every unit.

Does a pool heat pump need its own circuit?

Yes. A pool heat pump gets a dedicated 240-volt circuit, sized from the unit's data plate.

Does a pool heat pump need a disconnect?

Yes. Pool equipment needs a maintenance disconnect within sight of the equipment and at least 5 feet from the water, unless there's a permanent barrier between them.

Does a pool heat pump need GFCI protection?

We put GFCI protection on the 240-volt circuits we run to pool heat pumps, and we follow the code and the maker's install manual.

Why does my pool heat pump keep tripping the breaker?

Common electrical causes are loose or corroded connections at the pad, a failing breaker, a damaged wire, or moisture in the disconnect or whip. Sometimes it's the unit itself. Don't keep resetting the breaker, and don't put in a bigger one. Text us photos of the breaker, the disconnect and the data plate.

Does the heat pump have to be bonded?

Yes. We tie the unit into the pool's bonding as the code requires.

Do I need a permit to install a pool heat pump?

The electrical work for a new pool heat pump circuit is permitted work. We handle the permit with the building department that covers your address when the job needs one.

Can my electrical panel handle a pool heat pump?

We check your panel's free spaces and your home's overall load first. If the panel is full, old or undersized, we explain the options, such as a subpanel or a panel or service upgrade.

Do you work with my pool company?

Yes. We're the electrical trade partner for local pool contractors on new builds and equipment changes.

Which heat pumps do you install?

We install all major brands of pool heat pumps, including GulfStream, and wire each one to its installation manual.
