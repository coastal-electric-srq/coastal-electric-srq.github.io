Pool & spa electrical in Lakewood Ranch

Pool equipment is most of what we do in Lakewood Ranch. We handle the wiring and controls for pool pumps and equipment, pool light circuits, pool equipment subpanels and pool heat pump circuits. For Pentair automation, such as IntelliCenter, we wire, set up and program it.

Underwater pool lights that run above the low-voltage contact limit need GFCI protection (NEC 680.23(A)(3)). We check the light's circuit and protection when we work on it.

Equipment pads sit outdoors in heat, rain and sprinklers, so enclosures, connections and conduit fittings wear over time. We look at the whole pad, not just the one part that failed.
